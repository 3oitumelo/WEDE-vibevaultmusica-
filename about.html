About VibeVault Musica

Our Story.

Established in 2025, VibeVault Mausica was created as a physical pop-up and online community music hub based in Alexandra (Alex). We were founded to create a vibrant space where people gather to experience their favourite music, explore the rich history of iconic artists, and connect over shared sounds.

Our Mission.

To bring people together music by offering an interactive space where music lovers can explore gear, discover artist histories, and connect with their favourite tunes.

Our Vision.

To become Alexandra's primary cultural and music hub, inspiring emerging talent and uniting the local community through shared musical discovery.

Who We Serve.

Our Community includes:

  • Music enthusiast.
  • High school and university students.
  • Beginner musicians.
  • Local artists residing in Alexandra, and neighboring Johannesburg townships.
  • Music lovers across South africa via our web platform.
Why VibeVault Musica?

As a newly launched startup in Alex, we start with a clean slate. That means a modern, mobile first platform built directly for community engagement. Our website bridges the physical and digital space, allowing you to check event schedules, learn about music history, and order gear online.
